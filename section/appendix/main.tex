\section{Additional Evaluation}
\label{app:eval}

\rev{This appendix reports the counterfactual-\emph{quality} studies that complement the runtime results of Table~\ref{tab:multimodal_comparison}. All studies are reproducible (fixed seeds) and, consistent with the prototype, use synthetic embeddings; the counterfactual-generator comparison uses our own reference reimplementations of each method's core mechanism (not the released libraries, and not UCI data). Extending to large real corpora and released libraries on standard benchmarks (Adult, German-Credit, HELOC) is ongoing.}

\rev{\noindent\textbf{Counterfactual capability (Table~\ref{tab:baselines}).} We compare DcCA against reference reimplementations of representative counterfactual generators on validity, feasibility (immutability), in-distribution rate, set diversity, and cost. Data-grounded methods (DcCA, FACE) are in-distribution by construction, but only DcCA also respects immutability (via immutability-matched retrieval); model-centric generators achieve low nominal cost yet are infeasible and off-manifold. DcCA also yields the most diverse valid set. This is a complementary trade-off: model-centric methods minimize nominal cost, while DcCA emphasizes feasibility, realism, and verifiability.}
